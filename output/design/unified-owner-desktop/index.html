Counter Desk Desktop

One owner workspace for shop management, informed job review and deliberate local printing.

Nine design concepts, not application implementation. Records, rates, devices and online/payment states are illustrative. Written workflow and permission requirements override generated text. No automatic printing from scanning, opening a job or reconnecting.
