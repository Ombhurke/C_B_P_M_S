# CBPMS hero background options

Generated 7 October 2026 using the built-in image-generation tool. These are standalone background assets; no application theme, layout, or component was modified.

Direction: warm peach, apricot, ivory and taupe; understated photographic still lifes related to paper and printing; soft focus and generous space for headline overlays; no baked-in text; no blue or violet palette. The user-supplied screenshot informed the visual direction. Its cool upper tones were intentionally replaced with warm neutrals to follow the user's color constraint.

All four PNGs are 1672 × 941 pixels (approximately 16:9). Original generation outputs were copied without modification into `apps/web/public/images/hero-options/` and their SHA-256 hashes checked. These PNGs are selection masters; optimize delivery and verify text contrast when implementing the eventual theme.

My first choice for a restrained homepage is 01; choose 03 when the printer itself should explain the product. Native mobile/portrait crops have not been generated. The selection does not start the frontend redesign.
